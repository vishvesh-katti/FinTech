     - Maps variants: "Debit"/"DR"/"Withdrawal Amt" → unified "withdrawal"
     - Maps variants: "Credit"/"CR"/"Deposit Amt" → unified "deposit"

     - Duplicate transaction detection (same date + amount + narration)

     - A 0–100 "data confidence score" shown to user
     - Warns if <3 months of data (projections unreliable)
     - Warns if large cash withdrawals detected (untracked spending)

──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  2B. CASH FLOW INTELLIGENCE — WHERE IS YOUR MONEY FLOWING?
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

After categorization, a multi-layer analysis answers the key question:
"Where exactly is your money going?"

  FLOW ANALYSIS 1: Proportional Spend Breakdown
  - % of total outflow for each top-level category
  - Compared against national urban averages (hardcoded benchmarks from RBI data)
  - Flags categories where user is overspending vs. benchmark
  - Example: "You spend 34% on Food & Dining vs. 15% urban average — ₹8,400 excess/month"

  FLOW ANALYSIS 2: Fixed vs. Variable vs. Discretionary Decomposition
  - Fixed: Non-negotiable, date-bound obligations (rent, EMIs, insurance)
  - Variable-Necessary: Fluctuates but essential (groceries, fuel, medicine)
  - Discretionary: Lifestyle and want-based (dining out, OTT, shopping)
  - The "Leakage Score" = Discretionary ÷ Total Outflow × 100

  FLOW ANALYSIS 3: Merchant Intelligence
  - Top 10 merchants by total spend (bar chart)
  - Merchant frequency vs. amount matrix (are you spending often or in bursts?)
  - "Subscription Audit": All recurring fixed-amount charges listed separately
    with monthly total — many users are unaware of 6–8 active subscriptions

  FLOW ANALYSIS 4: Temporal Spending Patterns
  - Day-of-week heatmap (do you overspend on weekends?)
  - Week-of-month pattern (salary week vs. end-of-month crunch)
  - Month-over-month trend per category (is food delivery spend rising?)
  - Peak spending day identification

  FLOW ANALYSIS 5: UPI vs. Card vs. Net Banking Breakdown
  - Payment mode mix analysis
  - High-value UPI transfers flagged for manual review (possible informal lending)
  - ATM/cash withdrawals tracked separately as "Untracked Spend"

  FLOW ANALYSIS 6: Income Stability Analysis
  - Salary regularity check (same day each month? same amount?)
  - Side income detection (irregular credits from non-employer sources)
  - Income volatility score (critical for freelancers)
  - YoY income growth estimate if >12 months of data available

──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  3A. FINANCIAL HEALTH SCORE (0–850, like a credit score)
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  Scored across 6 dimensions (weighted):

  1. SAVINGS RATE (25%)
     - Monthly savings ÷ Monthly income
     - Benchmark: <10% Poor | 10–20% Fair | 20–35% Good | >35% Excellent
     
  2. DEBT SERVICE RATIO (20%)
     - Total EMIs ÷ Monthly income
     - Benchmark: <20% Healthy | 20–35% Caution | >35% Stressed
     
  3. EXPENSE DIVERSIFICATION (15%)
     - Herfindahl-Hirschman Index applied to spending categories
     - Extreme concentration in one category = risk signal
     
  4. INCOME STABILITY (15%)
     - Coefficient of variation of monthly income
     - Regular salary = lower risk = higher score
     
  5. INVESTMENT ACTIVITY (15%)
     - Are SIPs/MFs/investments detected in the statement?
     - Score drops to 0 if no investment activity found
     
  6. EMERGENCY FUND ADEQUACY (10%)
     - Estimated liquid balance vs. 6x monthly fixed expenses
     - Personalised (not static ₹25,000)

  OUTPUT: A circular gauge showing score + letter grade (A+ to F)
  + A "Score Improvement Roadmap" with 3 specific steps

──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  3B. DEPLOYABLE SURPLUS CALCULATION (Advanced)
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  CURRENT BALANCE
  = Total credits − Total debits (from statement)

  LOCKED CAPITAL BREAKDOWN:
  
  a) Fixed Obligations Reserve (exact, from AI categorization)
     = Sum of all is_fixed_obligation = true transactions × 1 month buffer
  
  b) Variable Expense Reserve (dynamic)
     = Average monthly variable spend × 1.20 (20% safety buffer)
     (old app used flat 15%, now personalised to spending volatility)
  
  c) Emergency Fund (personalised)
     = 3× average monthly fixed expenses (not static ₹25,000)
     (shown as a range: 3-month minimum to 6-month recommended)
  
  d) Tax Liability Reserve (new)
     = Estimated advance tax or GST if self-employed signals detected
     = 0 for salaried users with TDS already deducted
  
  e) Short-Term Goal Reserve (new)
     = User optionally tags upcoming large expenses (vacation, gadget, wedding)
       which are ring-fenced from investable capital

  DEPLOYABLE SURPLUS = Current Balance − (a + b + c + d + e)

  THREE SURPLUS SCENARIOS SHOWN:
  • Conservative Surplus: Using 6-month emergency fund
  • Base Surplus: Using 3-month emergency fund
  • Optimistic Surplus: If top spending recommendations adopted

──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  5B. MARKET DATA PROCESSING
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  For each asset, the following metrics are computed from 5 years of daily data:

  RETURN METRICS:
  • CAGR (5-year, 3-year, 1-year — to show trend)
  • Annualised Return (simple average)
  • Best Year / Worst Year (shows range of experience)
  • Maximum Drawdown (worst peak-to-trough loss ever seen in data)
  • Current vs. 52-week high/low positioning

  RISK METRICS:
  • Annualised Volatility (standard deviation of daily returns × √252)
  • Sharpe Ratio = (CAGR − risk-free rate) ÷ Volatility
    [Risk-free rate = current 91-day T-bill rate, fetched or hardcoded at 6.5%]
  • Sortino Ratio (downside deviation only)
  • Beta vs. Nifty 50 (systematic risk)
  • Value at Risk (95% VaR — "In a bad month, you could lose X%")

  CORRELATION MATRIX:
  • Full N×N correlation matrix across all assets
  • Displayed as a colour-coded heatmap
  • Used to identify diversification opportunities (low/negative correlations)
  • Highlight: Gold typically shows negative correlation with equity in downturns

──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  5C. PORTFOLIO CONSTRUCTION ENGINE
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  METHOD: Risk-Profile-Driven Allocation with Optimisation overlay

  STEP 1 — Base Allocation by Risk Profile
  
  Allocation Grid (% of deployable surplus):
  
  Asset Class         | Ultra-Con | Conservative | Moderate | Growth | Aggressive
  --------------------|-----------|--------------|----------|--------|----------
  Indian Large Equity |    5%     |    15%       |   30%    |  45%   |   50%
  Indian Mid Equity   |    0%     |     5%       |   10%    |  15%   |   20%
  Global Equity       |    0%     |     5%       |   10%    |  10%   |   10%
  Debt/Fixed Income   |   60%     |    45%       |   25%    |  10%   |    5%
  Gold                |   20%     |    15%       |   10%    |  10%   |    5%
  Silver              |    5%     |     5%       |    5%    |   5%   |    5%
  REIT                |    0%     |     5%       |    5%    |   5%   |    5%
  Liquid/Cash Buffer  |   10%     |     5%       |    5%    |   0%   |    0%

  STEP 2 — Efficient Frontier Optimisation (simplified)
  • Monte Carlo simulation: 5,000 random portfolio weight combinations
  • Each portfolio scored on Sharpe Ratio
  • Optimal portfolio = max Sharpe on the efficient frontier
  • Final allocation = blend of risk-profile base + optimised weights (60/40 blend)
  • This avoids pure black-box quant output while adding sophistication

  STEP 3 — Goal-Horizon Adjustment
  • Goals <3 years: Tilt toward debt/liquid (capital preservation)
  • Goals 3–7 years: Balanced tilt
  • Goals >7 years: Equity tilt (time diversifies volatility)
  • If multiple goals at different horizons, a blended allocation is shown

  STEP 4 — Tax Efficiency Overlay
  • ELSS funds flagged for users in 30% tax bracket (80C benefit)
  • Debt fund taxation note (indexation post-2023 rule change)
  • REIT dividend taxation (taxable at slab) vs. equity (LTCG at 10%)
  • Recommendations include post-tax return estimates

  OUTPUT: A recommended portfolio with:
  - Exact ₹ amount per asset class
  - Specific instrument suggestion per slot (ETF/index fund names)
  - Expected blended CAGR (weighted average)
  - Expected blended volatility
  - Blended Sharpe Ratio

  6A. GROWTH PROJECTIONS (Multi-Scenario)
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  For each goal, projections are run across 3 scenarios:

  SCENARIO 1 — BEAR CASE
  • Each asset's return = CAGR − 1 standard deviation
  • Represents an unfavourable but plausible market environment
  • Labelled: "Pessimistic (Bad decade)"

  SCENARIO 2 — BASE CASE
  • Each asset's return = 5-year historical CAGR
  • Labelled: "Expected (Historical average)"

  SCENARIO 3 — BULL CASE
  • Each asset's return = CAGR + 0.5 standard deviation
  • Represents a favourable market environment
  • Labelled: "Optimistic (Strong decade)"

  FOR EACH SCENARIO:
  - Year-by-year projected corpus table (expandable)
  - Final corpus vs. goal target (₹ gap or surplus)
  - % probability of hitting goal (derived from Monte Carlo distribution)
  - Inflation-adjusted real value of projected corpus

  LUMP-SUM vs. SIP COMPARISON:
  - If user deploys surplus as lump sum: show projection curve
  - If user deploys as monthly SIP (surplus ÷ 12): show SIP projection
  - Comparison: which strategy wins for the given time horizon?
  - (Lump sum wins in rising markets; SIP wins in volatile/falling markets)

──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  6B. MONTE CARLO STRESS SIMULATION
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  10,000 portfolio simulations over the investment horizon:
  - Each simulation randomly draws monthly returns from a distribution
    calibrated to each asset's historical mean and volatility
  - Includes rare crash events: 5% probability of -30% year (black swan)
  - Output: Distribution of final corpus values

  DISPLAYED AS:
  • Probability cone chart (fan chart):
    - 10th percentile (worst 10% outcomes)
    - 25th percentile
    - 50th percentile (median)
    - 75th percentile
    - 90th percentile (best 10% outcomes)
  • "Probability of Success" metric: % of simulations that hit the goal
  • Minimum surplus needed for 80% probability of success
  • "Sleep-well-at-night" portfolio: what allocation gives 90%+ probability?

──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  6C. MARKET CONTEXT & REGIME ANALYSIS
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  CURRENT MARKET CONDITIONS (fetched via yfinance):
  • Nifty 50 P/E ratio (if available via API or hardcoded latest)
  • Nifty 50 P/B ratio
  • Market cap to GDP ratio (Buffett Indicator for India)
  • Gold/Nifty ratio (relative valuation)
  • USD/INR trend (impacts global equity ETF returns)
  • FII/DII flow trend (net buyer/seller signal — if data available)

  MARKET REGIME CLASSIFICATION (rule-based):
  • BULL MARKET: Nifty above 200 DMA, P/E < 25
  • FROTHY: Nifty above 200 DMA, P/E > 25 (caution flag)
  • BEAR MARKET: Nifty below 200 DMA
  • RECOVERY: Nifty crossed 200 DMA from below (buying opportunity)

  Based on regime, a contextual advisory note is shown:
  • "Markets are currently frothy. Consider staggered deployment (STP) 
    rather than lump sum. Gold allocation looks attractive at current 
    Gold/Nifty ratio."

  6D. PERSONALISED ACTION PLAN

  A priority-ordered checklist of specific actions:

  IMMEDIATE (This Week):
  □ Cancel identified unused subscriptions (₹X savings)
  □ Set up auto-sweep FD for liquid buffer (earn 6.5% on idle cash)
  □ Check and set credit card auto-pay to avoid interest charges

  SHORT-TERM (This Month):
  □ Open a Zerodha/Groww/Kuvera account if not already invested
  □ Set up SIP of ₹X in [recommended ELSS fund] for 80C benefit
  □ Deploy ₹Y lump sum into [recommended liquid fund] as emergency buffer
  □ Review and potentially refinance [loan type] (estimated savings ₹Z/month)

  MEDIUM-TERM (This Quarter):
  □ Initiate diversified portfolio deployment per recommended allocation
  □ Set calendar reminder for portfolio rebalancing (6 months from now)
  □ Purchase/review term insurance coverage (if gap detected)

  LONG-TERM (Annual Review):
  □ Step up SIP by 10% each year to match salary growth
  □ Rebalance portfolio to target allocation (if any asset drifts >5%)
  □ Reassess risk profile and goals after major life events

──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  6E. PEER BENCHMARKING
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  Compare user's financial health against anonymised cohort data:

  Cohort defined by:
  - Income bracket (±20% of user's estimated income)
  - Age bracket (derived from statement duration, if multi-year)
  - City tier (metro / tier-2)

  Shown metrics (percentile rankings):
  • "Your savings rate (22%) is in the top 31% of your peer group"
  • "Your discretionary spend (38%) is higher than 67% of similar earners"
  • "Your investment activity score is in the bottom 40% — most peers have 
    active SIPs by this income level"

  NOTE: All peer data is synthetic/benchmark-based (RBI, SEBI, AMFI publications)
  — no real user data is aggregated.
